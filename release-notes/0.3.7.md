- **Remove, renumber or add cues.** Click any cue, after recording or on a song already in Prime, to change its slide number, remove it, or add the next slide after it. Arrow keys move it or pick another, and Delete removes it.
- **Catches more slips.** Going to a slide too early and back again no longer leaves a stray cue: the app keeps the click after you went back, and shows the early one struck through in case you want it. One section change clicked well off the beat no longer throws off the rest of the song.
- **Easier to read.** The selected cue now has a light highlight with clear buttons, the same on the review screen and in Saved Cues.
- **The recording list shows the newest slide in full** at the bottom as it comes in.
