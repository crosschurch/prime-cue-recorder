## What's new

- **Edit cues already in Prime.** Choose a song with lyric cues to see where each slide fires, with its words. Click a slide and use − / + (or ← / →) to move just that one.
- **Move a group at once:** every slide, the lyrics only, or only Interlude and blank slides. Bring lyrics in a touch early while instrumental slides wait. The same controls are on the review screen after recording.
- **Slide words are right for songs with an arrangement.** Slides past a repeated Chorus used to show as Blank with no group. Your saved cues were firing the right slides.
- **The recording list grows downward** and follows the newest slide, like a chat.
- Shift Lyric Cues in the toolbar now always moves the whole week; use a song's Saved Cues for one song.
