## What's new

- **Shift Lyric Cues** (the ⇆ button in the toolbar): move every saved lyric cue in this week's songs, or just one song, earlier or later at once. It's 0.5 seconds earlier by default, for when slides keep changing a little late.
- Playlist item cues stay where they are, Prime is backed up first, and shifting back the other way undoes it.
