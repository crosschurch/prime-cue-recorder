- **Slide words match the playlist's arrangement.** When a song in the ProPresenter playlist uses a different arrangement from the song's own (like "Full" instead of "Master"), the app now reads that one, so every slide shows its words instead of "Slide 25".
- **Connects to ProPresenter on its own.** If ProPresenter isn't open yet or is slow to answer when the app starts, the app keeps trying every few seconds instead of waiting for you to open Settings.
